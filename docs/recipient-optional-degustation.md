# Giveaways without a known recipient

Open **Sales → Dégustations & exchanges → Issue Dégustation**. This action is available in the General Manager's default all-employees history view as well as an individual view. The history filter does not select the employee recording a new giveaway; the form confirms that employee separately. Read-only report roles retain their existing restrictions.

Choose the finished products and quantities, enter a reason or reference, and confirm. **Recipient (optional)** can remain **No specific customer** for tasting samples or giveaways whose recipient is unknown. The employee, authenticated recorder, products, quantities, date and reason remain in the history.

The existing checked company-stock transaction consumes available finished-product lots at their recorded production carrying value. It debits the configured marketing Dégustation expense and credits the corresponding inventory account. Selling price and catalogue cost estimates do not determine that expense. It preserves lot provenance and residual cents, checks stock availability and the accounting period, and prevents duplicate deductions on retries.

Previously expensed promotional allocations retain their separate selection and recipient controls. Recording those giveaways consumes the allocation without posting a second expense. Deploying this source does not change past giveaways or company balances.
