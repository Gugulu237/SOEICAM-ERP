# Income statement presentation

The income statement displays **Total Sales Revenue** with the standard gross profit, operating profit and net profit / loss labels. Desktop, mobile, PDF/print and Excel omit the VAT notice, basis selector, tax breakdown and accounting-result comparison.

The approved calculation is unchanged: posted net revenue plus the actual sales-VAT legs from the selected dated journal snapshot supplies total sales revenue. That same component continues to flow into the displayed profit figures. It is not exposed as a separate report line or report mode.

Primary revenue and export rows show the aggregate sales amount. Exact posted revenue accounts remain accessible under the collapsed **Ledger account details** panel, with their original journal drilldowns, separately from the aggregate report figure.

Printing/PDF and Excel capture the selected dates before refreshing their ledger snapshot and recompute totals from that fresh evidence. Missing evidence produces unavailable totals and disables export with a plain sales-total message, rather than guessing or silently reusing stale figures.

This is a report presentation. It does not rewrite sales, VAT liability, stock costs, journal entries, cash flow or balance-sheet retained earnings.
