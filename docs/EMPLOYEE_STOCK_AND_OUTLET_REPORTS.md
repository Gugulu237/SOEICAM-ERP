# Approval history, employee goods and outlet retail reports

Approval history is available from the sidebar and from the Approvals screen. Choose a date range (Cameroon business dates), search by reference or person, filter by record type, and export the visible results as CSV. The General Manager sees the company history. Other active staff see records allowed for their role and their own records. Completed payment records remain visible after posting or reversal. Server audit events retain their decision details; older approval metadata is labelled separately. Missing approvers are never invented. Amount and current status describe the current source document; the history is not a totals ledger.

Use Inventory → Issue goods to select an active employee and a source warehouse, then the finished products and quantities. Employees with a hôtesse template cannot receive sale stock. Custom job profiles resolve to their current operational template. Driver and vehicle are independent optional fields. Without a vehicle, the system provisions an empty stock location belonging to the employee; it does not adopt unreconciled legacy custody. Issuing goods transfers lot quantities and values without posting revenue. Open-run and residual-stock checks prevent mixing allocations.

Employees open My issued stock to see their own saleable quantities and record a cash sale, credit sale, exchange or combined supply. The server binds the salesperson to the signed-in employee and rejects another employee’s run, company warehouse selection, and arbitrary invoice authority. Existing company Sales permissions remain separate. Cash sales remain cash in transit until received through the existing cashier/receipt approval workflow. Warehouse management counts and receives unsold goods, including quarantined exchange returns, then reconciles and closes the run.

Hôtesses see products actually delivered to their assigned outlet as an image grid. Tap a product, enter units sold now, and save. Each save appends an informational report and updates reported remaining stock. A changed stock snapshot requires refresh; retries do not duplicate reports. No company sale, invoice, receipt, stock movement, customer balance or journal is written by retail reporting. The closing report starts with the units already reported today, cannot reduce those totals, and still requires a physical closing count. Closing the day blocks additional retail updates; later deliveries may still receive a shelf check.

These changes use the existing records and extension fields. No database migration or historical stock rewrite is required. Deploy the updated main branch and rebuild the Next.js application. Source publication alone does not confirm the live deployment.
